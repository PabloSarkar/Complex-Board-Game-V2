ZONE COMMANDER

A deck-building tactical board game

◈ Control the golden ZONES along the equator to earn Control Points. First to 25 CP wins.

◈ Each player fields 20 units in 4 squadrons of 5 (one ★Commander each).

◈ Move, Split and Converge squadrons — each action costs 1 AP (20 AP per turn).

◈ Capture 🏘 Villages, ⚡ Energy Wells and ⛏ Mines. Leave garrisons to defend them.

◈ Landing on an enemy squadron starts an ENCOUNTER — pick every unit's move, then watch them resolve in agility order. Moves cost ⚡Power.

◈ You control BOTH sides (testing mode). Pan: drag / WASD · Zoom: wheel · Cancel: Esc.
